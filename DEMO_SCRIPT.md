# BizDesk: 3-Minute Video Demo Script

**Speaker**: Prachi Zala  
**Target Audience**: Technical Evaluators, Small Business Owners  

### [00:00 - 00:30] Small Business Friction
"Hello! I'm Prachi Zala. Independent clinics, salons, and tutors struggle with fragmented paper diaries and WhatsApp messages. This leads to double-booked clients and unbilled revenue. BizDesk is an agile, all-in-one operations and billing platform built specifically for them."

### [00:30 - 01:30] Collision-Proof Booking Engine
"Our appointment calendar prevents overlapping time slots through atomic interval validation. When an appointment is scheduled from 10:00 to 10:45, our algorithm automatically rejects overlapping attempts, guaranteeing zero double-bookings."

### [01:30 - 02:30] Invoicing & PyMuPDF Vector Generation
"Once a consultation finishes, BizDesk computes itemized totals, GST tax rates, and discounts. With one click, our PyMuPDF engine generates an elegant, print-ready vector PDF invoice complete with business tax IDs and customer details."

### [02:30 - 03:00] Stack & Verification
"Engineered with FastAPI, SQLAlchemy, and PyMuPDF, fully verified with automated pytest suites. Thank you!"
